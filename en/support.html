Getting started

  • See it on a real lecture first. On first launch tap Try the Sample Course, or open Courses › Sample Course. It has slides, a short recording with its transcript, notes, a summary and a quiz. Delete it like any course when you’re done.
  • Add your slides. Open a course and tap Add Slides, drag files in, or use Open in Notemory from Files or Mail. PDF, PowerPoint and Word files work.
  • Record a lecture. In a deck tap the Record button. The transcript is made on your iPad as the lecturer speaks; write on the slides while it runs.
  • Hear a moment again. Tap the Listen button (the ear), then tap your handwriting: playback jumps to when you wrote it.

Common questions

Is my lecture audio uploaded?

No. Recordings and transcripts stay on your iPad (and in your own iCloud if you turn on sync). Speech is turned into text on the device.

Which languages are transcribed?

Turkish and English. The first recording in a language may download Apple’s speech model; keep the iPad online for that.

Do I need an account?

No. AI features use an anonymous account the app creates after you accept its AI notice. Signing in with Apple (Settings › Account) is optional and keeps your plan and AI allowance on all your devices.

What do the plans include?

Free has 3 notebooks and 3 documents with recording, transcription, annotation and search. Lite removes those limits, without AI. Plus and Pro add the AI features (summaries, quizzes, the tutor and more) with a monthly allowance. The yearly Plus and Pro plans start with a 7-day free trial; monthly plans have no trial. Settings › AI Plan and Usage shows your plan and how much of the month’s allowance you have used.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Settings › AI Plan and Usage › Manage Subscription, or your Apple Account’s Subscriptions settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep the plan until the period ends. Notebooks and documents are never deleted when a subscription ends.

I reinstalled the app or use a new iPad. Where is my subscription?

Tap Restore Purchases on the plans screen or in Settings › AI Plan and Usage, signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I move my notes to another iPad?

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on both iPads, or export a backup in Settings › Backup and restore it on the other one.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Account › Delete Account. Your notes stay on your iPad; see the Privacy Policy for what is deleted on our server.
