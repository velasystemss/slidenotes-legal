Who we are

Notemory is an iPad app published by Gokberk Şenel (Vela Systems), Prof. Dr. Ahmet Taner Kışlalı Mah., Selçuk Yula Cad., Çankaya/Ankara, Türkiye (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the data described here under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: velanotemory@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your slides, notebooks, ink, recordings and transcripts stay on your iPad (and in your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync). We don’t have a copy.
  • Lecture audio never leaves your device. Speech is turned into text on the iPad.
  • AI features (Plus and Pro) send only the text and images a feature needs, after you accept a notice, to our server in Frankfurt and from there to Anthropic’s Claude API in the United States. We don’t store that content.
  • There are no ads in the app. We advertise the app on Meta (Facebook, Instagram) and TikTok. Only if you allow it in Apple’s tracking prompt do those networks learn, with your device’s advertising identifier, that you installed, opened or subscribed after seeing one of our ads; if you don’t, nothing is sent to them. Your notes, recordings, transcripts and anything you write are never shared with them.
  • You don’t give us your name. AI features use an anonymous account made on your device; signing in with Apple is optional.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings › Account › Delete Account.

What stays on your device

Imported slides and documents, notebooks, handwriting, text boxes, stamps, images, audio recordings, transcripts, quizzes, flashcards, review history, courses, exams, assignments, study plans and settings are stored on your iPad. Search indexing, handwriting and text recognition for search (Apple Vision), speech recognition (Apple’s on-device speech engines), slide matching for imported recordings, graphing and the audio overviews’ voice are processed on the device.

iCloud (optional). If you turn on iCloud sync, your library is stored in your own iCloud account (CloudKit and iCloud Drive) under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We cannot read it.

Calendars and notifications (optional). If you allow it, the app writes exams, assignments and study blocks to a Notemory calendar and reads the times (never the titles) of events in the calendars you choose, on the device. Reminders are local notifications. School calendar links you add are stored in your iPad’s keychain and downloaded directly from the school’s server; they are never sent to us.

AI features (Plus and Pro)

Nothing is sent before you accept the AI notice in the app. When you use an AI feature, the app sends only what that feature needs, for example: the text of your slides and transcripts (and images of slides that have little text), your typed notes and marks, images of handwriting you ask to be read, your question and the passages from your notes it is answered from, a photo or file of an exam schedule, or the text you ask the writing assistant to change. Audio is never sent.

This content goes to our server functions hosted by Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany, region eu-central-1) and from there to Anthropic PBC’s Claude API in the United States, which produces the answer. Our server does not store the content of requests or answers and does not write it to logs; it records only what is needed to run the service and enforce plan allowances: an anonymous user id, which feature was used and when, token counts and cost, run and batch identifiers, and error codes. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use inputs and outputs of its API to train its models, and deletes them from its systems within 30 days, except content flagged for violating its Usage Policy (kept for up to 2 years) or data it must keep to comply with the law.

Anonymous account. On the first AI use the app creates an anonymous account with Supabase Auth. Its session is stored in your iPad’s keychain. It has no name, email or password.

Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose Sign in with Apple in Settings › Account, Apple gives us a stable identifier for your Apple Account and an email address: your own, or a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”. We don’t ask Apple for your name. Supabase Auth stores them with your account so your AI plan, usage and subscription follow you to your other devices and after reinstalling. Your notes are not part of the account.

Advertising measurement (Meta and TikTok)

We run ads for Notemory on Meta’s platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Audience Network) and on TikTok. To learn which ads lead to installs and subscriptions, the app contains their software development kits (SDKs). Never sent to them: your notes, slides, recordings, transcripts, handwriting, questions, AI content, course or file names, your email or our account id.

Only with your permission. After onboarding, iOS asks whether Notemory may track you (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you allow it do Meta’s and TikTok’s SDKs start, and only these events go to them, with your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA): that the app was installed or opened and that onboarding ended (from the SDKs), and that a free trial started or a subscription was bought or renewed, with its price and currency (to Meta from RevenueCat, to TikTok from our server). For this, RevenueCat stores your advertising identifier, the identifier for vendor, your IP address and Meta’s identifier for the app with your subscription. TikTok’s SDK may also send TikTok crash reports about its own code while it runs.

If you don’t allow it (or haven’t answered yet), nothing is sent to Meta or TikTok: neither SDK starts, no advertising identifier is read, RevenueCat receives none of these identifiers, and neither RevenueCat nor our server sends them any trial, purchase or renewal. The only signal is Apple’s own: the app tells iOS which stage you reached (installed, finished onboarding, started a free trial, subscribed) as a number, and if you came from an ad, Apple’s AdAttributionKit / SKAdNetwork sends that ad network an anonymous, delayed report that doesn’t identify you or your device. This happens for everyone, whatever you answer, and nothing in it comes from us directly.

Changing your mind. You can allow or stop tracking at any time in iPadOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking (or Settings › Notemory). When you stop it, the SDKs stop collecting at once and don’t start again, and RevenueCat records the change so no further trial, purchase or renewal is sent. You can also ask Meta and TikTok about data they received under their own privacy policies (Meta, TikTok), which also say how long they keep it.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your anonymous account id, the App Store transaction and subscription details (product, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, trial, country/currency) and basic app and device information needed to validate purchases. RevenueCat informs our server of subscription changes so the right plan applies to your account; our server stores the subscription’s state, product, dates, trial status and event ids (not prices or payment details).

Transfers outside Türkiye and the EU

AI requests are processed by Anthropic and subscription data by RevenueCat in the United States. If you allow tracking, advertising measurement data goes to Meta and TikTok, which process it in the United States and other countries; that transfer relies on your explicit consent. The other transfers rely on the safeguards available under KVKK Article 9 and GDPR Chapter V (such as standard contractual clauses). Our server provider stores data in the EU (Germany).

How long we keep data

Usage records are kept for 12 months for allowances, billing disputes and statistics, then deleted or anonymised. Subscription records are kept as long as the subscription exists and afterwards as long as the law requires. Request content is not kept by us. Data on your iPad stays until you delete it or the app. When you delete your account, see “Deleting your account” below.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent (KVKK Article 11; GDPR Articles 15–22). Because the account is anonymous, include the Account ID shown in Settings › AI Plan and Usage when you write to velanotemory@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or the data protection authority in your EU country.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings › Account › Delete Account. This deletes at once, on our server: your account, its Apple sign-in identifier and email, your AI usage records, run and batch identifiers and any plan override, and removes your account id from our subscription event log. If you signed in with Apple, the app asks Apple to confirm and we revoke Notemory’s Sign in with Apple tokens; you can also remove Notemory in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

Two things stay for a limited time, without anything that leads to you: if you held an App Store subscription, its record stays (it belongs to the purchase, so a later restore can move it to a new account, and as a financial record), and so do the usage counts of its current month or free trial (numbers of runs, tokens and cost per day, never content), so that deleting and restoring can’t reset the subscription’s AI allowance. Those counts are deleted automatically once that month or trial is over; the deleted account’s random id is removed from the subscription record 30 days after the subscription ends.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your Apple Account’s Subscriptions settings (the Delete Account screen links there). Your notes are not on our server: they stay on your iPad and, with iCloud sync on, in your iCloud. Delete the app to remove them from the iPad, and delete Notemory’s data in your iCloud storage settings to remove them from iCloud. You can also ask us to delete your data by email, with the Account ID from Settings › AI Plan and Usage.

Children

Notemory is made for students in higher education and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Connections to our server use TLS. The Anthropic API key exists only on the server. Session tokens are kept in the iPad’s keychain.
