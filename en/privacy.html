Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Who we are

SlideNotes is an iPad app published by Gokberk Şenel (Vela Systems), Prof. Dr. Ahmet Taner Kışlalı Mah., Selçuk Yula Cad., Çankaya/Ankara, Türkiye (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the data described here under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: velalabss@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your slides, notebooks, ink, recordings and transcripts stay on your iPad (and in your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync). We don’t have a copy.
  • Lecture audio never leaves your device. Speech is turned into text on the iPad.
  • AI features (Plus and Pro) send only the text and images a feature needs, after you accept a notice, to our server in Frankfurt and from there to Anthropic’s Claude API in the United States. We don’t store that content.
  • There are no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics.
  • You don’t give us your name or email. AI features use an anonymous account made on your device.

What stays on your device

Imported slides and documents, notebooks, handwriting, text boxes, stamps, images, audio recordings, transcripts, quizzes, flashcards, review history, courses, exams, assignments, study plans and settings are stored on your iPad. Search indexing, handwriting and text recognition for search (Apple Vision), speech recognition (Apple’s on-device speech engines), slide matching for imported recordings, graphing and the audio overviews’ voice are processed on the device.

iCloud (optional). If you turn on iCloud sync, your library is stored in your own iCloud account (CloudKit and iCloud Drive) under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We cannot read it.

Calendars and notifications (optional). If you allow it, the app writes exams, assignments and study blocks to a SlideNotes calendar and reads the times (never the titles) of events in the calendars you choose, on the device. Reminders are local notifications. School calendar links you add are stored in your iPad’s keychain and downloaded directly from the school’s server; they are never sent to us.

AI features (Plus and Pro)

Nothing is sent before you accept the AI notice in the app. When you use an AI feature, the app sends only what that feature needs, for example: the text of your slides and transcripts (and images of slides that have little text), your typed notes and marks, images of handwriting you ask to be read, your question and the passages from your notes it is answered from, a photo or file of an exam schedule, or the text you ask the writing assistant to change. Audio is never sent.

This content goes to our server functions hosted by Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany, region eu-central-1) and from there to Anthropic PBC’s Claude API in the United States, which produces the answer. Our server does not store the content of requests or answers and does not write it to logs; it records only what is needed to run the service and enforce plan allowances: an anonymous user id, which feature was used and when, token counts and cost, run and batch identifiers, and error codes. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use inputs and outputs of its API to train its models, and deletes them from its systems within 30 days, except content flagged for violating its Usage Policy (kept for up to 2 years) or data it must keep to comply with the law.

Anonymous account. On the first AI use the app creates an anonymous account with Supabase Auth. Its session is stored in your iPad’s keychain. It has no name, email or password.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your anonymous account id, the App Store transaction and subscription details (product, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, trial, country/currency) and basic app and device information needed to validate purchases. RevenueCat informs our server of subscription changes so the right plan applies to your account; our server stores the subscription’s state, product, dates, trial status and event ids (not prices or payment details).

Transfers outside Türkiye and the EU

AI requests are processed by Anthropic and subscription data by RevenueCat in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards available under KVKK Article 9 and GDPR Chapter V (such as standard contractual clauses). Our server provider stores data in the EU (Germany).

How long we keep data

Usage records are kept for 12 months for allowances, billing disputes and statistics, then deleted or anonymised. Subscription records are kept as long as the subscription exists and afterwards as long as the law requires. Request content is not kept by us. Data on your iPad stays until you delete it or the app.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent (KVKK Article 11; GDPR Articles 15–22). Because the account is anonymous, include the Account ID shown in Settings › AI Plan and Usage when you write to velalabss@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or the data protection authority in your EU country.

Deleting the app removes everything on the device. To delete what our server holds about your anonymous account, contact us. Subscriptions are cancelled in your Apple Account’s settings.

Children

SlideNotes is made for students in higher education and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Connections to our server use TLS. The Anthropic API key exists only on the server. Session tokens are kept in the iPad’s keychain.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and note the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
